`diagnose_wind_update` derives changed atoms, both-evaluation query supports, a typed DAG with bin/horizon guards, structural and numerical supports, affected tasks, task closure and reassignment neighborhood. Unknown suffix evaluations conservatively query all bins along their fixed geometry. `validate_recovery_plan` combines structural, commitment, permission, mission and reserve results and reports only a feasible level upper bound on success. No failed candidate excludes a level. The original low-level scope API remains available for explicit conservative supports.

`PathLibrary` freezes nominal/return polylines and binds them to a scenario hash. `search_level` lazily enumerates assignment-consistent sequences, paths and reserve choices, then compiles scalar transfers into continuous affine constraints. Exact rational elimination produces checked primal or Farkas evidence; HiGHS only proposes numerical solutions. `recover` searches original labels in order and shares identical release spaces. A live cursor continues enumeration and retries unresolved LP branches; saved counters are replay positions, not trusted serialized cursors. Certificates use exact rational transfers for scope membership, even when their float projections equal incumbent quantities. `previous_premise` distinguishes simulator-tolerance feasibility from exact-decimal feasibility of the incumbent before the update. No scalable-performance or unrestricted-path optimality claim is established.
